DocNum Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16/10/2026

DocNum ("the app", "we", "us") helps patients book a doctor's queue token or appointment from home, and helps clinics run their daily queue. The app is operated by Abhishek Kumar Yadav, Vill - Khaira, P.O - Phatra, P.S - Mayurhand, Chatra, Jharkhand, India, 825408.

DocNum is a booking and queue tool. It does not give medical advice, make diagnoses, or store medical records, prescriptions or test results.

1. Information we collect

WhatWho it is aboutWhy we need it
Name and mobile numberPatientsTo create your account, log you in with a one-time password (OTP), and let the clinic identify you.
Name, email address and passwordDoctors, receptionists, administratorsTo create and secure your account. Passwords are stored by our authentication provider in hashed form; we cannot read them.
Name and age of the person a token is forPatients, or family members booked by a patientSo the clinic knows who each token belongs to.

Clinic detailsClinicsClinic name, address, phone number and which doctors and staff work there.

Activity recordsAll usersA log of important actions (for example a doctor being approved, a booking being cancelled, a session being closed), recording who did it and when. It is used for security and accountability.

What we do not collect: your device location, your contacts, payment or card details, medical records or diagnoses, or an advertising ID. The app does not show ads.

Because a booking shows that a person sought care from a particular doctor or clinic, we treat booking information as sensitive.

2. How we use your information

  • To create your account and verify your mobile number or email.
  • To make, show, change and cancel bookings, and to run the clinic queue.
  • To tell you about your bookings and queue progress inside the app.
  • To check and approve doctors before they appear to patients.
  • To keep the service secure, investigate problems and prevent misuse.
  • To produce counts and statistics (for example how many bookings a clinic had this week) for administrators and doctors.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Who can see your information

  • You can see your own account and bookings.
  • The doctor and receptionists at the clinic you book with can see the name, age and mobile number linked to bookings for their own sessions, so they can run the queue.
  • Administrators can see doctors' contact details while reviewing their registration, aggregate reports, and the activity log. The activity log and reports are designed to contain identifiers, statuses and counts rather than patient names or phone numbers.
  • Other patients cannot see your information. They can see which token numbers are already taken, but not who holds them.
  • Everyone using the app can see an approved doctor's public profile and the clinic's address.

5. How long we keep information

  • Account details: until you delete your account.
  • Booking records: 2 years after the visit date, unless you ask for earlier deletion.
  • Activity records: 1 year.
  • Backups: removed on the provider's normal backup cycle after deletion.

6. Deleting your account and data

Patients and receptionists can delete their account inside the app: open Profile and tap Delete my account. This permanently removes your profile, your notifications and your bookings, and cancels any upcoming tokens or appointments. It cannot be undone.

Doctors, and anyone who cannot use the app, can ask us to delete their account. Email krishnabhi04092026@gmail.com from the email address on the account (or include the registered mobile number) with the subject "Delete my DocNum account", or use the page at delete-account page. We will verify the request and complete it within 30 days. Doctor accounts take longer because their sessions, and patients' bookings linked to them, have to be dealt with first.

When an account is deleted we keep one minimal record that it happened: an anonymous account ID, the account type and the time of deletion. We keep it for security and accountability. We may also keep other limited records where the law requires it, and we will tell you if that applies.

7. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Our database restricts each user to the information their role needs, and important administrative actions are logged. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children

DocNum accounts are meant for people aged 18 and over. A parent or guardian may book a token for a child by entering the child's name and age. We do not knowingly create accounts for children.

9. Your choices and rights

You can correct your name and mobile number in the app under Profile. You can ask us to access, correct or delete your information, or to stop using it, by contacting us below. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under applicable law, including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
