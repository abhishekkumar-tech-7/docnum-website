DocNum

Book a doctor's queue token or appointment from home, and skip the long wait at the clinic. Built for clinics in small towns and cities across India.

Android app: closed testing now — Google Play launch coming soon.

What DocNum does

  • For patients: find a doctor, pick a queue token (or a time slot), see live queue progress, and get reminders.
  • For doctors and clinics: open sessions, manage the daily queue, call the next patient, and see simple reports.
  • Fees: doctors set their own consultation fee. DocNum adds a small platform fee for online booking. Fee details are always shown before you confirm a booking.
DocNum is a booking and queue tool. It does not give medical advice, diagnoses or prescriptions. In an emergency, go to the nearest hospital.
