Delete your DocNum account

You can ask us to delete your DocNum account and the personal information linked to it. You do not need the app installed.

How to delete your account

Patients and receptionists: open the DocNum app, go to Profile, and tap Delete my account. The deletion happens straight away.

Doctors, or if you cannot open the app:

  1. Send an email to krishnabhi04092026@gmail.com with the subject Delete my DocNum account.
  2. Write from the email address on your account, or include the mobile number you registered with.
  3. Tell us whether you are a patient, doctor or clinic staff member.

We may reply to confirm it is really you before we delete anything, and we will complete the request within 30 days. Doctor accounts take longer because their sessions, and patients' bookings linked to them, must be handled first.

What is deleted

  • Your account and login details
  • Your profile (name, mobile number, email)
  • For doctors: your public profile, photo and professional details
  • Your notifications and your bookings, including the names and ages entered for them

What we may keep

We keep one minimal record that the deletion happened: an anonymous account ID, the account type and the time. It is kept for security and accountability. We may also keep other limited records where the law requires it.

Please note

  • Deletion cannot be undone.
  • Upcoming bookings are cancelled when your account is deleted.
  • Backups are cleared on the provider's normal backup cycle.
