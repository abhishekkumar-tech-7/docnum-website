About us

DocNum is operated by Abhishek Kumar Yadav, Vill - Khaira, P.O - Phatra, P.S - Mayurhand, Chatra, Jharkhand, India.

We started DocNum to cut waiting time at clinics. Patients book a token from their phone and see how the queue is moving; doctors and receptionists run the day from one simple screen.

Nature of service

DocNum is an online booking service. We deliver no physical goods, so no shipping is involved. A booking is confirmed instantly in the app once complete.
